Color rules: a 9-step warm gray scale gives every role its own step: background, sunken wells, cards, elevated sheets, hairlines, placeholder, metadata, secondary text, ink. Dark mode lightens surfaces for depth (cards sit above the background, sheets above cards) and switches shadows off. Semantics stay out of the brand: cobalt leads the interface, green means alive or success, amber means expiring, and red is reserved for recording-in-progress and errors, so red always demands attention. Try it: the mic opens the creation sheet; "Flip all themes" toggles dark mode. Animations respect reduced-motion. Single self-contained HTML file; fonts from Google Fonts, photo from picsum.photos.
